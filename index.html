NoakhaliVoice AI

An AI-powered speech emotion recognition application for Noakhali Bangla dialect. The project applies WavLM-based modeling to analyze speech and predict emotional categories from uploaded or recorded audio.

  • WavLM
  • ~2,300 Audio Samples
  • 25 Participants
  • Speech Emotion Recognition
  • Undergraduate Thesis Project
  • WavLM
  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • Speech Emotion Recognition
  • Gradio

Problem: Low-resource Noakhali Bangla speech presents challenges for emotion recognition because of limited dialect-specific data.

Built: A web-based speech emotion recognition system that accepts uploaded or recorded speech and predicts emotional categories.

Technology: WavLM, Python, PyTorch, Gradio and machine learning.

Result: Developed as an undergraduate thesis project with a parameter-efficient WavLM-based approach.
